Corrections Policy

We correct errors promptly and transparently.

  • Material errors are fixed in the article body and noted with a correction timestamp.
  • Minor typographical errors may be fixed without a note.
  • If new verified information changes a story materially, the article is updated with an "Updated" timestamp explaining what changed.
  • Articles found to be fundamentally unsupported are removed, with a removal notice retained where appropriate.

Report errors via our Contact page, including the article URL.
