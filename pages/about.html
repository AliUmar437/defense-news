About Us

Defense News is an independent digital news publication covering international military developments, defense technology, military aviation, naval forces, ground forces, defense procurement, military exercises, and international security.

Our editorial mission is to deliver timely, informative, source-attributed coverage. Every article links to its original reporting so readers can verify claims at the source.

We are not affiliated with any government, military, or defense contractor. We do not use official insignias or imply official endorsement.

How we work

  • Stories are collected from reputable public sources: official defense ministry announcements, established news organizations, and respected defense publications.
  • Every story is validated by our editorial process before publication.
  • Uncertain or disputed claims are held for editorial review and never published as established fact.
