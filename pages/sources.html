Source & Attribution Policy

What we publish from

Official defense ministry announcements, official military press releases, government and international organization statements, reputable international news organizations, authorized RSS feeds, and established defense publications. Our configured sources are listed in config/news_sources.json in the public repository.

How we attribute

  • Every article names its source publication and links directly to the original reporting.
  • Images carry credits and source information.
  • We never copy full source articles or lightly rewrite copyrighted reporting — we publish original summaries.
  • We never present two outlets repeating one original report as independent confirmation.

What we don't do

No scraping in violation of terms, no paywall bypassing, no hotlinking images without permission, no fabricated citations.
