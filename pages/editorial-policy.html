Editorial Policy

Accuracy first

Headlines must be supported by sources. Confirmed facts are kept separate from analysis, speculation, and unverified claims. We never invent quotations, casualty figures, military movements, weapons specifications, official statements, or eyewitness accounts.

Sources

We prioritize official announcements and reputable news organizations. Every article names its sources and links to the original reporting. See our Source & Attribution Policy.

AI assistance

AI may assist with news discovery, summarization, and drafting. AI-generated or AI-assisted articles are labeled as such. AI confidence scores never decide truth on their own; disputed or high-impact claims require human review and, where possible, independent corroboration.

Sensitive stories

Casualty reports, disputed battlefield claims, and major geopolitical allegations are held for editorial review unless supported by sufficient reliable evidence.

Corrections

Errors are corrected promptly and transparently under our Corrections Policy.
