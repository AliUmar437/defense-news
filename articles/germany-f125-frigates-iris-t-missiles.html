Germany Finally Gives Its F125 Frigates Real Teeth with IRIS-T Missiles

By Robert HayesOct 02, 2026 21:01 UTC 1 min readRegion: Europe

Germany is finally giving its F125 frigates some real firepower, fitting the 7,200-ton warships with IRIS-T SLM surface-to-air missiles.

The F125s were built with notoriously light armament for ships their size — a design choice that drew years of criticism, The War Zone reports. Adding IRIS-T SLM, a capable medium-range air defense missile, partly addresses the gap.

IRIS-T SLM is the surface-launched version of the IRIS-T air-to-air missile, with a vertical-launch variant offering 360-degree engagement against aircraft, cruise missiles, and drones. It has seen combat in Ukraine, where German-supplied systems have performed well against Russian air attacks.

The upgrade reflects Germany's broader naval rearmament. After decades of underinvestment, Berlin is now spending heavily on its fleet — new frigates, submarines, and missile upgrades across the board.

For the F125s, designed for stabilization missions rather than high-end combat, the missiles are a step toward credibility in contested waters.

Installation timelines have not been disclosed.
