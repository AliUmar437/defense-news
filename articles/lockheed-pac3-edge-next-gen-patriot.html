Lockheed Unveils PAC-3 Edge, Its Next-Generation Patriot Interceptor

By Robert HayesOct 09, 2026 19:45 UTC 1 min readRegion: United States

Lockheed Martin has unveiled the PAC-3 Edge, its next-generation Patriot interceptor — and is putting hundreds of millions of dollars of its own money behind it.

The company plans to invest heavily in the missile itself, plus its seeker and solid rocket motor, Tim Cahill, president of Lockheed's missiles and fire control division, said, according to Breaking Defense. Company-funded development on this scale signals Lockheed's confidence in long-term demand.

The PAC-3 is the Patriot system's hit-to-kill interceptor, designed to destroy incoming ballistic missiles, cruise missiles, and aircraft by slamming into them directly. It has seen real combat in the Middle East and Ukraine, where Patriot batteries have become among the most valuable — and most targeted — air defense assets in the world.

The "Edge" variant promises improved performance against evolving threats, including maneuvering hypersonic weapons that stress current interceptors.

With global demand for air defense at historic highs, Lockheed is betting the PAC-3 franchise has decades of growth ahead.
