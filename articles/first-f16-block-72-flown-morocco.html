First F-16 Block 72 Takes Flight, Seven Years After Morocco's Order

Robert HayesOct 06, 2026 17:09 UTC 1 min readRegion: Global

The first F-16 Block 72 has taken to the air, seven years after Morocco became the launch customer for the variant.

The Block 72 is the latest evolution of the F-16, distinguished by its Pratt & Whitney F100 engine and the same advanced avionics as the Block 70: the APG-83 AESA radar, a modern glass cockpit, and the structural upgrades that extend the airframe's life to 12,000 hours.

Morocco's order kicked off the Block 72 line, and the long wait reflects both production ramp-up and the complexity of standing up a new variant. The flight marks the beginning of deliveries to Rabat, which is modernizing its air force amid regional tensions.

The F-16 production line in Greenville, South Carolina, now has a backlog stretching years into the future, with multiple countries waiting on new Vipers even as the design approaches its 50th anniversary.

For Lockheed Martin, every Block 70/72 delivery is proof the Viper remains commercially alive well into the stealth era.
