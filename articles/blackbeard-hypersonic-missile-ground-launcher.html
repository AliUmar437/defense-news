Army Shows 'Blackbeard' Hypersonic Missile Firing from Ground Launcher

Michael ReynoldsOct 08, 2026 16:32 UTC 1 min readRegion: United States

The U.S. Army has shown its Blackbeard hypersonic missile being fired from a mobile ground launcher, offering a rare public look at one of its newest strike programs.

Blackbeard is part of the Army's push to make hypersonic weapons more mobile and more affordable, The War Zone reports. Traditional hypersonic programs have been expensive and tied to large, fixed infrastructure; Blackbeard aims to put the capability on trucks that can hide, move, and shoot.

Hypersonic missiles fly at more than five times the speed of sound while maneuvering, compressing enemy reaction time to minutes. The Army wants them for striking high-value targets — air defenses, command posts, ships — deep inside contested territory.

A ground-launched hypersonic weapon also fills a gap left by decades of treaty limits. With those constraints gone, the Army is building a family of long-range fires from PrSM ballistic missiles up through hypersonics.

The footage does not reveal the missile's range or speed, and the Army has not announced a fielding date.
