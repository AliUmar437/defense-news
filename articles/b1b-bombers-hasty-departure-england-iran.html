B-1B Bombers Beat a Hasty Retreat from England Amid Iran Threats

Michael ReynoldsOct 05, 2026 01:04 UTC 1 min readRegion: Europe

B-1B Lancer bombers have made a hasty departure from RAF Fairford in England, after Iran openly threatened the base that hosted American bombers during Operation Epic Fury.

The sudden exit, reported by The War Zone, underscores how seriously the Pentagon takes Iranian threats against the facilities supporting its bomber operations. Some B-1s had remained at Fairford after the operation; the threats prompted a rapid dispersal.

The B-1B, America's supersonic conventional bomber, played a visible role in the strikes on Iran, flying long-range missions with heavy payloads of cruise missiles and guided bombs. Its presence in England was itself a signal — and Iran's threats were the response.

Dispersing bombers is standard practice when bases are threatened, but the speed of this move suggests the threat was considered credible and imminent.

The bombers' current location has not been disclosed.
