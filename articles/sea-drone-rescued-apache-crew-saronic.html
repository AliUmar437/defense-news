Sea Drone That Rescued Downed Apache Crew Hailed by Pentagon Chief

James CarterSep 18, 2026 22:05 UTC 1 min readRegion: United States

Secretary of War Pete Hegseth visited Saronic Technologies in Austin, Texas, to meet the team behind the unmanned surface vessel that helped rescue a downed Apache helicopter crew.

The visit, part of Hegseth's "Arsenal of Freedom" tour spotlighting the defense industrial base, highlighted a rescue that has become a signature story for military unmanned systems: a sea drone reaching crew members in distress where manned rescue might have been slower or riskier.

Saronic builds autonomous surface vessels designed for the Navy's hybrid fleet — small, attritable, and increasingly autonomous. The company's rise mirrors the Pentagon's broader bet that unmanned systems will multiply the fleet's reach without multiplying its cost.

The Apache rescue demonstrated the other side of that bet: drones not just as weapons, but as lifesavers.

Hegseth's tour has focused on companies outside the traditional defense primes, signaling where the Pentagon wants innovation to come from.
