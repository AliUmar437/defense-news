USCYBERCOM Wraps 13th Annual Cyber Flag Exercise with Global Partners

By Defense News StaffSep 08, 2026 06:41 UTC 1 min readRegion: United States

U.S. Cyber Command has concluded its 13th annual Cyber Flag exercise, wrapping nearly three weeks of intense, simulated cyber combat alongside partners and allies from around the world.

The exercise, held in Suffolk, Virginia, ended July 31, 2026, according to the Defense Department. Teams faced simulated malicious cyber threats designed to test their ability to defend military networks under realistic, high-pressure conditions.

Cyber Flag has grown into the premier exercise for the U.S. cyber mission force, bringing together operators from across the services and allied nations. This year's iteration also gathered senior military cyber leaders for a multi-national symposium on the sidelines — a sign of how central coalition cyber operations have become.

Real-world stakes back the training. State-linked hackers probe U.S. and allied networks daily, and exercises like Cyber Flag are where defenders rehearse the coordination those attacks demand — across agencies, services, and borders.

Cyber Command has not released details of the scenarios used, citing operational security.
