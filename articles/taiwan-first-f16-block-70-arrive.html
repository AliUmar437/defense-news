Taiwan's First F-16 Block 70 Fighters Finally Arrive

David SullivanOct 02, 2026 16:34 UTC 1 min readRegion: Indo-Pacific

Taiwan has received its first two F-16 Block 70 fighters, the long-delayed start of a major expansion of the island's fighter fleet.

The delivery, reported by The War Zone, begins the fulfillment of an order placed years ago that has been repeatedly pushed back. For Taipei, the new jets — the most advanced F-16 variant ever built, with an AESA radar and modern avionics — are a badly needed reinforcement.

Taiwan's air force flies one of the oldest fighter fleets in the region, and its pilots scramble constantly to shadow Chinese military aircraft operating around the island. Beijing has steadily increased the scale and intensity of those operations, and Taiwanese defense planners have warned that the airframes they have are wearing out under the tempo.

The Block 70s, often called the F-16V, bring Taiwan's Vipers close to fifth-generation standards in sensors and networking, even without stealth. They will join an ongoing upgrade of Taiwan's existing F-16A/B fleet to a similar standard.

Further deliveries are expected to continue over the coming years, though the exact pace has not been made public.
