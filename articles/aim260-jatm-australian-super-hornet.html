Secretive AIM-260 Missile Spotted on Australian Super Hornet

James CarterOct 01, 2026 17:01 UTC 1 min readRegion: Indo-Pacific

The secretive AIM-260 Joint Advanced Tactical Missile has been photographed carried by an Australian F/A-18F Super Hornet — a rare public sighting of the weapon meant to replace the AMRAAM.

The photos, reported by The War Zone, show the long-range air-to-air missile on a Royal Australian Air Force jet. For Australia, the AIM-260 promises what its air force needs most: reach. Against Chinese fighters armed with very-long-range missiles, Australian Hornets and future aircraft have been outranged.

The AIM-260 has been in development for years under unusual secrecy, with the Air Force disclosing little about its range or capabilities. It is designed to outrange the AIM-120D AMRAAM it replaces, restoring the beyond-visual-range advantage.

Australia's access to the missile signals the depth of U.S.-Australian airpower integration under AUKUS-era cooperation.

Neither government has commented on delivery timelines.
