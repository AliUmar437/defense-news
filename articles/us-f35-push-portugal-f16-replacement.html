U.S. Pitches F-35 as Portugal Weighs Replacing Its F-16s

Robert HayesOct 08, 2026 20:14 UTC 1 min readRegion: Europe

The United States is lobbying hard for Portugal to choose the F-35 Lightning II as the replacement for its aging F-16 fighters, as Lisbon weighs cost, capability, and strategic alignment.

Portugal's decision, reported by The War Zone, pits the American stealth fighter against European rivals. For a mid-size NATO air force, the choice is about more than jets — it determines decades of industrial partnerships, training pipelines, and interoperability.

The F-35's pitch is its sensor fusion and stealth, which have made it the default fifth-generation choice for most U.S. allies; more than a dozen countries now fly or have ordered it. European competitors counter with lower operating costs and promises of industrial workshare.

Portugal currently flies F-16AM/BM aircraft bought second-hand and upgraded years ago. Whatever Lisbon picks will anchor its airpower through the 2060s.

No decision timeline has been announced, but the American push suggests Washington sees the competition as genuinely contested.
