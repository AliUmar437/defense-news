GDLS to Deliver New PERCH 2.0 Loitering Munition Prototypes to Army This Month

By Defense News StaffOct 09, 2026 16:35 UTC 1 min readRegion: United States

General Dynamics Land Systems is set to deliver four prototypes of its new PERCH 2.0 loitering munition platform to the Army by the end of October, putting fresh hardware into soldiers' hands within weeks.

The prototypes are headed to the 1st Brigade, 1st Cavalry Division under an existing contract, a company executive told Breaking Defense. Getting early versions to an operational brigade lets the Army test the system the way it would actually be used — and feed fixes back before larger buys.

Loitering munitions — essentially one-way attack drones that circle over the battlefield until they find a target — have become one of the most consequential weapons of the Ukraine war. Both sides have used them by the thousands against armor, artillery, and air defenses, and every major army is now racing to field its own.

PERCH 2.0 is GDLS's bid for a slice of that demand from the U.S. Army, which has been moving urgently to equip brigades with organic drone strike capability rather than relying solely on higher-echelon assets.

The company has not disclosed the munition's range, warhead size, or unit cost.
