U.S. Delivers $6 Million in Long-Range Drones to Panama for Border Security

Michael ReynoldsSep 17, 2026 16:48 UTC 1 min readRegion: Global

The United States has delivered four long-range K1000 unmanned aircraft — valued at more than $6 million — to Panama's Ministry of Public Security, expanding security cooperation aimed at halting smuggling and transnational threats.

U.S. Ambassador Kevin Marino Cabrera handed over the drones, funded by U.S. Southern Command, with a clear stated purpose: stopping threats and illegal smuggling routes long before they approach the American homeland, according to the Defense Department.

The K1000 is a long-endurance unmanned system built for persistent surveillance over remote terrain — exactly the environment of Panama's Darien region, a notorious corridor for drug trafficking and migrant smuggling.

The delivery reflects Washington's strategy of pushing security outward: equipping partners to interdict threats at the source rather than at the U.S. border.

Panamanian forces will operate the aircraft with American training and support.
